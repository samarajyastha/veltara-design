About Veltara Consultants

Empowering students to achieve their international education dreams since 2010.

Our Story

Founded in 2010 by a group of international education enthusiasts, Veltara Consultants began with a simple mission: to make quality global education accessible to students from all backgrounds.

What started as a small consultancy in a single office has now grown into a trusted name with thousands of successful student placements across top universities worldwide.

Our team of dedicated consultants brings together decades of combined experience in international education, university admissions, and career counseling.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission

To provide personalized, ethical guidance that helps students navigate the complex journey of studying abroad, ensuring they find the best academic and career opportunities tailored to their aspirations.

Vision

To become the most trusted education consultancy globally, recognized for transforming lives through education and creating a community of globally competent professionals.
